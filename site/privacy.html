Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

The short version

The Cleanup Assist desktop app collects nothing and sends nothing. It has no network code in it at all — no analytics, no crash reporting, no update checks, no accounts. Everything it reads about your disk stays on your machine.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Its host keeps standard server logs, and the web fonts are served by Google, which means Google sees your IP address when the page loads. That's the whole list.

Who this covers

This policy is published by Richard Gaskin (RGaskinLtd), who develops Cleanup Assist. It covers the Cleanup Assist desktop application and this website. It does not cover third-party services you may reach from here, each of which is named below with a link to its own policy.

The desktop application

Cleanup Assist is a local disk analyzer. To do its job it reads information from your computer, and none of it is transmitted anywhere.

What it reads

  • File and folder metadata — names, paths, sizes and timestamps for the locations you choose to scan. It does not read the contents of your files.
  • Installed application records — the Windows uninstall registry keys and Microsoft Store package folders, so it can tell you which application owns a folder.
  • Drive information — which drive letters exist and how much free space they have.

What happens to it

It is held in memory while the app is running, shown to you, and discarded when you close the app. Cleanup Assist has no servers, no database, and no network requests of any kind. Scan results are not written to disk, not uploaded, and not shared with anyone — including the developer.

The app changes files only when you explicitly tell it to. When you move a folder to another drive, that operation happens entirely on your own machine.

Children

Cleanup Assist is a general-purpose system utility and is not directed at children. No personal information is knowingly collected from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because neither the app nor this site collects personal data that identifies you, there is no account to access, correct or delete, and no data to export. The only records that exist are the short-lived server logs described above, held by the host.

If you are in the UK or EU and believe data relating to you has been handled improperly, you may contact the developer using the details below, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
