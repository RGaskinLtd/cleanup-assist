What the software does to your files

This section matters more than the rest. Cleanup Assist is a tool for reclaiming disk space, which means it can permanently change, move and delete data on your computer.

The application is built to be cautious. It never acts on its own: every deletion and every move is something you explicitly start. It refuses to touch operating-system folders. When it relocates a folder to another drive it keeps the original until the copy and the junction that replaces it have both been verified, and it rolls back if any step fails. Nonetheless:

  • Keep current backups. No amount of care in the software substitutes for a backup you have tested.
  • You decide what is safe to remove. The safety badges and rules are informed guidance, not guarantees. A folder labelled reclaimable may still hold something you want; a folder labelled unclassified may be entirely disposable. Read the explanation the app gives before you confirm anything.
  • Moving a folder changes your system. Relocating a folder and leaving a junction behind works transparently for the great majority of software, but some applications, backup tools and anti-cheat systems behave differently when they meet a reparse point.
  • Run it with the access it needs. Running as administrator lets the app see more of your disk, and equally lets it act on more of your disk.
